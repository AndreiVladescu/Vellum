Search inside your books without a server, on desktop.

* Translate a selected passage on the device, with packs you keep.
* Goodreads and StoryGraph imports keep your ratings, shelves, read
  dates and reviews — not just the titles.
* A Series page showing which volumes you are missing.
* Tap an author to see the rest of their books.
* Overdue loans are counted where you can see them.
* Free catalogues (Gutenberg, Wikisource) in the OPDS browser.
